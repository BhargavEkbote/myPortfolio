LetsChat's Current State

Date: August 21st, 2026

So obviously I haven't updated this blog and this portfolio site as a whole, and there are multiple reasons to it.

Over the past few years I haven't been into web development as much, and as a result I haven't worked on LetsChat in that whole time, though I do want to get back to developing LetsChat again.

So the plan is to slowly get back into developing it, and I plan to add blogging functionality to LetsChat, and later integrate it into NovaChat so that this blog no longer needs to serve as the destination for LetsChat related news/updates, and instead this blog will remain a spot for all non-LetsChat stuff.

Because even though LetsChat is a personal project, it has become much bigger than the rest of my projects, so it makes sense to have a separate blog for it.

Though, until that update comes, this blog will temporarily remain the destination for all LetsChat updates.
